answers.

Is Offscreen available yet?+

Not yet. We're currently building and testing the first version with a small group of users and sponsors.

Joining early access means you'll be among the first invited to try it.

Where do the rewards come from?+

Sponsors fund eligible focus sessions.

We're testing different reward formats and amounts to find something that's valuable for users while remaining sustainable for sponsors.

Am I watching ads while I focus?+

No. That's the point.

Sponsor attribution happens around the session, not through banners or interruptions while you're trying to work.

How do you know I'm actually working?+

We won't pretend we can read your mind.

Offscreen can measure whether you follow the rules of a focus session, but it can't know whether you spent every second writing code instead of staring out the window. The product is designed to encourage genuine focus rather than claim something we can't verify.

Why would a company pay me to focus?+

Because instead of buying another banner or interruption, a relevant company can fund a positive experience for someone who may actually care about its product.

A developer tool might sponsor coding. An education company might sponsor studying. A writing tool might sponsor writing.

You get rewarded. They get remembered for helping you focus.
