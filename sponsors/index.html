fake sessions?

It's one of the most important problems we're testing.

Early safeguards may include limits on rewarded sessions, minimum payout thresholds, account and device controls, and behavioral signals.

But we won't sell sponsors a claim we can't substantiate.
Offscreen does not promise

“We scientifically verified this person worked productively for 25 minutes.”
